# The Mega-Campaign

A multi-year multiplayer journey through five Paradox grand strategy games — Imperator: Rome → Crusader Kings III → Europa Universalis IV → Victoria 3 → Hearts of Iron IV. Roughly thirty players, persistent identities, one continuous world from 304 BCE to 1948 CE.

Jump to: [Description](#description) · [Rules](#rules) · [Contact](#contact) · [Timeline](#timeline) · [Maps](#maps)

---

## Description {#description}

The campaign chains five grand strategy games end-to-end via converters. Each subcampaign runs over several months of weekly sessions, with breaks between games for narrative interregnum and converter setup. Players keep persistent identities across the entire chain — no per-game wipe — with session attendance rate-limited by a credits system and supported by a load-bearing reserve list.

For the full design and locked decisions, see [Reference](reference/).
